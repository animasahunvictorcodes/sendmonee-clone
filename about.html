Our Company is passionate about fintech innovation

Especially when exploring peer-to-peer payments in particular. Our goal is to revolutionize the transfer of funds across Africa and internationally.

"We saw an opportunity to innovate how people send and receive money outside traditional banking methods," says CEO and Co-Founder Deji Soyemi. "As an easy alternative to visiting a bank branch or sending money electronically, we sought ways to facilitate direct transactions through an ecosystem that is not only easy to use but also envisions a scenario where successful transactions can occur with multiple currencies within one platform." he goes on to say.

Our solutions, your success

Our
Mission

We are on a mission to innovate social payments beginning with Africa and its diaspora and then expanding worldwide. Our goal is to close the gap between local and international money transfers by providing our users with simple and easy access to foreign currency and exchange whenever they need it.

Innovative global social
currency exchange

At SendMonee, We believe in giving control back to our users. Our platform uniquely allows users to quote their own exchange rates within regulatory standards. This feature ensures transparency and fairness in every transaction, separating us from conventional money transfer services.
