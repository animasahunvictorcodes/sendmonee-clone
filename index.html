Frequently asked questions

What is Sendmonee Marketplace?

Sendmonee Marketplace is a person to person platform for direct buying and selling of foreign currency, eliminating intermediaries for better rates and increased control over international transactions.

How do I list my wallet on Sendmonee Marketplace?

To list your wallet; On the Home Screen, select the "List Wallet" button, select the currency, set the minimum and maximum amounts that can be bought, choose the currency you want to receive, and enter your exchange rate.

Are there any fees for listing my wallet?

No, there are zero listing fees on Sendmonee Marketplace. You can maximize your profits without any upfront costs.

What is the minimum and maximum amount I can send and receive?

There’s no minimum limit for transfers—you can send or receive any amount. The maximum transfer limit depends on your account’s currency and can be viewed under Account limits in the Account & Security section of your profile tab.
